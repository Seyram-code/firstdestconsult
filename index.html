Short-Course Study Program Terms

Review these terms before applying. They apply to Study Visa opportunities and describe the service and payment stages.

Read the full terms and payment conditions

1. Services and decisions

First Dest Consult (“First Dest”) will assist with the agreed short-course application process, which may include helping identify or apply to a school, preparing and submitting application materials with your authorization, and assisting with preparation of a visa application. First Dest does not make admission or visa decisions and cannot guarantee admission, an offer of acceptance, a visa, or a particular processing time.

2. Fees and payment stages

  • Office registration — GHS 2,000: Payable to begin First Dest’s services. This fee is non-refundable under any circumstance, subject to rights that cannot legally be waived under applicable law. No other payment is due to First Dest before it begins the program application process on your behalf.
  • School application and admission — CAD 500: Payable after First Dest obtains an admission or invitation letter, or other written confirmation that the school has secured a place for you. This covers application and admission fees. After payment, the school is expected to issue its formal Offer of Acceptance, subject to the school’s requirements. Once the Offer of Acceptance is issued, First Dest will begin assisting with your visa application. This amount is non-refundable once First Dest has successfully secured admission for you. School-controlled fees remain subject to the school’s refund policy and applicable law.
  • Visa and biometrics — CAD 185: Payable when the visa application package is ready. Once paid, this amount is non-refundable, including if the application is refused, withdrawn, or unsuccessful, to the extent it has been paid to or is non-refundable by the relevant authority or provider.
  • Course fees and agency fee: Payable only after your visa is approved. The amounts, due dates, recipients, and applicable refund conditions must be provided in writing before payment is due. Unless agreed otherwise in writing, course fees are payable to the school or its designated provider.

3. Applicant responsibilities

You agree to provide complete, accurate, and genuine information and documents; review application materials and promptly correct errors; meet school and visa requirements and deadlines, including biometrics appointments; pay stated fees when due and keep receipts; notify First Dest of changes that may affect your application; and not request submission of false, altered, or misleading information. First Dest may pause its work if required information or payment is not provided on time, or stop acting if you provide false documents or request a false statement.

4. Third-party decisions, fees, and delays

The school and government authorities control their admission, fee, visa, and processing decisions. First Dest is not responsible for their decisions, delays, policy changes, or system interruptions. Any additional amount required by a school, authority, biometrics provider, or other third party must be disclosed before payment is requested, and your written approval should be obtained before a new or changed payment is made on your behalf.

5. Refunds and cancellation

Fees described as non-refundable are subject to applicable law and the terms of the relevant school or government service provider. If you cancel, withdraw, or stop participating, you remain responsible for third-party fees already paid or incurred, subject to applicable refund policies and law. No course fees or agency fee are payable before visa approval. Refund requests must be made in writing; First Dest will explain which amounts were paid to third parties and whether their policies allow a refund.

6. Communication and records

You authorize First Dest to communicate with the relevant school and service providers as reasonably necessary to provide the agreed services. Your information will be used for the application process and related communications, subject to applicable privacy requirements. Keep copies of this agreement, submitted documents, and payment receipts.

7. Changes and acknowledgment

This agreement and any written fee schedule or service description accepted by both parties form the agreement for this short-course application. Changes to fees, payment stages, or services must be made in writing and accepted by both parties.
